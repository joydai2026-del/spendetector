How it works

A receipt photo to a Notion row, step by step
1

Telegram delivers the photo to the Notion Worker

The bot's webhook points at the worker's Notion URL. The worker checks a secret header and that the sender is the allowed chat, then grabs the largest photo. A non-photo message gets a friendly nudge instead of dead air.

2

It replies "Got your receipt" immediately

Before the slow work begins, the bot acknowledges, so you are never staring at silence. It also hashes the image and checks Notion, so re-sending the same photo will not create a duplicate.

3

GPT-4o reads the receipt into structured JSON

The image goes to GPT-4o vision with a strict schema. It returns store, date, totals, and each line item with quantity, unit price, a category (1 of 8), a food group (1 of 10), and a health tier (green / yellow / red). Card digits are scrubbed in the prompt.

model: gpt-4o-2024-08-06 · ≈ $0.005 per receipt
4

It compares prices to your history

For each item it looks up past prices in the Items DB and flags any move over 10 percent. This is the shrinkflation engine. The biggest move becomes the receipt's headline insight.

5

It writes to Notion: one receipt row, one row per item

The Receipts DB gets the store and totals. The Items DB gets every line item with chart-ready fields (number, select, date, plus week and month buckets). This is what every chart on the dashboard reads.

6

It builds a fun report on the receipt's own page, then replies

Best-effort extras: a health grade, a "cozy pantry" report-card image, and 2 to 3 recipe ideas built from the actual items ("Cook This Tonight") with a menu illustration. Then the bot sends the final message with store, total, the top insight, and a tap-through Notion link.

report-card + menu images: gpt-image-1 · recipes: GPT-4o
⏱️
Reality check on timing: the full path takes about 95 seconds (OCR, writes, two images, recipes). That is why the demo opens in Notion and talks over the seeded dashboard while the new receipt processes in the background. Plan for it, do not fight it.

Technical setup

The stack, the data model, the deploy

Capture

Telegram Bot API. A dedicated bot. Photos only from your allowed chat ID, verified by a secret token.

Runtime

Notion Workers (@notionhq/workers), TypeScript, hosted by Notion. Deployed with the ntn CLI.

Intelligence

OpenAI GPT-4o vision for extraction, GPT-4o for recipes, gpt-image-1 for the report-card and menu images.

Store + visuals

Notion API. Two databases plus native chart views. Charts read plain fields, never formulas or rollups.

Privacy

Card digits scrubbed. OpenAI retention + training opt-out. Data lives in your private Notion. Export and delete are yours.

Cost

Worker runtime included with Notion. About $0.005 per receipt of vision. Roughly $0 to $10 a month total.

Q&A prep

Likely questions, crisp answers

Why not just use a budgeting app?

They categorize the bank transaction (one total). None read your line items. The line items are where habits and price creep actually live.

Is this a Notion Agent?

No. It is a Notion Worker, which is deterministic code Notion hosts. An Agent is an AI chat interface and cannot reliably build charts, buttons, or automations on its own. A Notion Agent could sit on top later as a "what got more expensive?" assistant.

Why move off Modal?

To put everything on one platform. Notion Workers runs the code next to the data and the dashboard. Fewer moving parts, and a cleaner Notion story. Modal still works and is kept as a fallback.

How accurate is the reading?

Around 90 percent on line items with GPT-4o. Low-confidence items are flagged, and you can fix any category or name directly in Notion, since it is just a database.

What about privacy and my card?

Card digits are stripped and never stored. Vision runs with retention and training opt-out. Everything lives in your private Notion workspace, and export or delete is one action.

What does it cost?

About half a cent per receipt for the AI. Worker runtime is included with Notion. Realistically $0 to $10 a month.

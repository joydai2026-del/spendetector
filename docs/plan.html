1. Why this wins

The real gap Copilot, Rocket Money, Cleo, and Spendee all categorize the bank total ("Walmart $87"), never the items. Only reward apps like Fetch read items, and they sell your data to brands. So the honest, unserved product is: read your line items, keep the data yours. Your one-line demo pitch writes itself: "your bank says $87 at Target. Mine says your shampoo quietly went up 18% since February."

What line items unlock that totals never can: per-item price history (shrinkflation made visible), hidden leaks (3 to 4 surprise ones surface within ~2 months of scanning), and real frequency and brand patterns ("coffee 4x this week").

Privacy (a differentiator) Receipts are sensitive (merchant, items, card last-4). Unlike Fetch, we never sell your data. Strip card digits, encrypt stored images, one-tap export and delete, and use OpenAI vision with retention/training opt-out.
